Family letter

The Sunday-night note scramble.

You have three reminders, one sweet classroom moment, and zero desire to format another family letter from scratch. Codex turns that pile into a finished note.

Problem

The notes are real. The format is the time sink.

You already know what families need to hear. The annoying part is making the note clear, warm, organized, printable, and not weirdly robotic.

Give Codex

Messy bullets are enough.

“Short vowels, math partners, mystery word wall, library Tuesday, sneakers Friday, kindness practice. Make it sound like me.”

Codex makes

A finished letter you can edit.

It drafts the message, adds structure, checks tone, formats the page, and leaves you with something you can print or paste into email.

Your rough input
  • Short vowel practice went well.
  • Math partner work was strong.
  • Ask families about the mystery word wall.
  • Library Tuesday. Sneakers Friday.
Codex plan

Keep the voice warm, make reminders easy to scan, add one conversation starter, and format the letter so it feels intentionally made.

Final product

A family note that looks done.

Family Note

Friday

Dear First Grade Families,

We had a busy, happy week in Room 104. Our readers practiced listening closely for short vowel sounds, and our mathematicians worked with partners to explain their thinking clearly.

Ask your child: “What happened at the mystery word wall?”
Next week

Library is Tuesday. Please return books if they are hiding in backpacks.

Quick reminder

Sneakers on Friday for gym and outdoor movement.

Warmly,
Ms. Rasmussen
