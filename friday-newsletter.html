Friday newsletter

A weekly update that does not look exhausted.

Instead of wrestling with a blank newsletter template, you drop in the week’s mess of notes. Codex turns it into a warm, scannable update.

Problem

The update matters, but the layout is a drag.

Families need reminders and a window into the classroom. You need something that looks put-together without becoming a graphic design project.

Give Codex

Dates, highlights, and the tone you want.

“Keep it warm. Mention word detectives, math partners, library Tuesday, sneakers Friday, and one at-home question.”

Codex makes

A newsletter with hierarchy.

It organizes the update into sections, makes reminders stand out, and keeps the voice from sounding like a district memo.

Final product

A Friday note families can skim.
