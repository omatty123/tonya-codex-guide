Sub plan

A guest teacher plan that lowers the chaos.

When you are sick or pulled into a meeting, the last thing you want is to reconstruct every classroom routine from memory. Codex can make the plan readable.

Problem

You know the routine. A guest teacher does not.

The classroom runs on tiny details: line order, helper jobs, where materials live, and which transition language actually works.

Give Codex

Schedule plus “stuff in my head.”

“Morning meeting, reading groups, math tubs, recess, quiet choices. Add helper names, calm language, and top-drawer sticker note.”

Codex makes

A plan someone can follow.

It turns your routines into a schedule, materials list, helper notes, and “if things go sideways” backup plan.

Final product

A calmer day on paper.
