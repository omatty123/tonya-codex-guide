Self-check page

A routine students can actually use.

You already repeat the same writing reminders every day. Codex can turn that routine into a clear self-check page that helps students pause before they turn work in.

Problem

The reminders are good. Saying them forever is not.

First graders need visual, repeatable cues. A self-check page lets the routine live on the table instead of only in your voice.

Give Codex

Your classroom language.

“Use finger spaces, reread your sentence, try your best spelling, add a picture detail. Make it first-grade friendly.”

Codex makes

A tool, not another lecture.

It creates a simple checklist, a confidence scale, and a teacher note area you can use for conferences.

Final product

A writing self-check page.

Before I turn it in

I Can Check My Work

Writing
I used finger spaces. I reread my sentence. I tried my best spelling. My picture has a detail that matches my words.
How did I do?

1 = I need help. 2 = I am getting it. 3 = I can teach it.

Teacher note

Use this for quick conferences or small-group follow-up.
