You know the skill. Codex can turn it into a printable worksheet, station activity, or quick check with directions and answer spaces already laid out.

Problem

The skill is simple. Making it cute takes forever.

A short-a practice page should not steal half a prep period. You need clean directions, enough repetition, and a layout first graders can follow.

Give Codex

A target skill and a few words.

“Short a CVC words. Include cat, map, fan, ran, sad, tap. Make a first grade worksheet with sorting and writing.”

Codex makes

Printable practice with structure.

It makes directions, word tiles, sorting boxes, handwriting lines, and optional challenge prompts without you nudging every rectangle into place.

Your rough input
  • Short a CVC words.
  • Read, tap, write routine.
  • Needs to work as independent station work.
  • Keep directions short.
Codex plan

Build one page with a name/date line, word tiles, a sort, writing lines, and a tiny “try one more” challenge for fast finishers.

Final product

A short-a sheet ready for stations.
